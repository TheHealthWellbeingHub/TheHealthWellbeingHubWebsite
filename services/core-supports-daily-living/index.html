NDIS Core Supports & Daily Living in Logan

Core Supports funding covers the everyday, practical help that makes independent living possible — personal care, household tasks, transport and, where needed, overnight support. We deliver this support in a way that respects your routines, your culture and your independence, not a fixed roster that suits us more than you.

Get started today Call 0433 604 507

Support workers are based in and around Logan Central and travel to participants across Logan, Brisbane and surrounding South East Queensland suburbs.

What are NDIS Core Supports?

Core Supports is the most flexible category of NDIS funding — it's designed for the day-to-day supports that help you live your daily life and work toward your goals, including assistance with daily life (personal care and household tasks), transport, and consumables. Exactly what's approved and how it can be used depends on your individual plan.

Care that respects your values

Gender-matched support workers available on request, dietary requirements and cultural needs respected, religious observances accommodated. Your values matter to us — whatever they are.

Who this service is for

  • Participants who want to stay living independently at home with the right support
  • Families wanting culturally and religiously appropriate in-home care for a loved one
  • Anyone who has been offered a generic roster by another provider and wants support that actually fits their routine

How our support workers work

We start by understanding your routine, your preferences and what dignity and independence look like for you specifically. Support is then scheduled around your life, with the same familiar faces wherever possible rather than a rotating roster of strangers.

Multilingual and culturally responsive support

Gender-matched support workers are available on request, dietary requirements and cultural needs are respected, and religious observances are accommodated as part of everyday support — not treated as a special request. Our team speaks Arabic, Somali, Dari and Amharic as well as English.

How to get started

  1. Call or submit an enquiry — tell us what daily support you need
  2. We call you back, usually within 2 business hours
  3. We complete a short needs assessment, in your preferred language if needed
  4. We match you with the right support worker(s)
  5. Support begins — often within the same week

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose a male or female support worker?
Yes. Many of our participants and families have a preference for this, and we do our best to match you with a support worker of your preferred gender.
Can support workers accommodate religious practices, like prayer times or halal meal preparation?
Yes — we ask about this as a standard part of onboarding, not as a special request, so your worker knows what to expect from day one.
Is overnight support included?
It can be, if your plan includes funding for it. Overnight support ranges from a sleepover to active overnight care, depending on your assessed needs.
How quickly can daily living support start?
In most cases we can begin within the same week as your first enquiry, once a needs assessment is complete.
