How the NDIS decides

Age

Under 65 on the day you apply.

Where you live

Living in Australia, as a citizen, permanent resident or Protected Special Category visa holder.

Disability

A disability that is likely to be lifelong, and makes everyday things hard without help.

Who decides

The NDIA. This check is a quick guide, not a decision.
