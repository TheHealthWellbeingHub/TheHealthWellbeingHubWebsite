Complaints & Feedback

How to raise a concern, give feedback, or make a complaint — and what happens next.

Last updated {{ current_year }}

We want to hear from you

Whether it's positive feedback or a concern, we want to know. Raising an issue never affects the support you receive from us.

How to give feedback or make a complaint

  1. Use the form on this page
  2. Speak with your support worker or coordinator directly, if you're comfortable doing so
  3. Call us on {{ site.phone_display }}
  4. Email us at {{ site.email }}
  5. Write to us at {{ site.address_full }}

You're welcome to raise a complaint in Arabic, Somali, Dari or Amharic — let us know your preferred language and we'll connect you with a team member who speaks it. You can also ask a family member, advocate or support coordinator to raise it on your behalf.

What happens after you raise a concern

  • We'll acknowledge your complaint and confirm we've received it
  • We'll look into what happened and, where appropriate, discuss it with the people involved
  • We'll get back to you with an outcome or next steps
  • We'll keep a record of the complaint and how it was resolved, in line with the NDIS Practice Standards

External complaints

If you're not comfortable raising a concern with us directly, or you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission:

  • Phone: 1800 035 544 (free call)
  • Website: ndiscommission.gov.au

You will never be disadvantaged for making a complaint, to us or to the NDIS Commission.
