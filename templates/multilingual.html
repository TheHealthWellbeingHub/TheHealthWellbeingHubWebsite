A team that speaks your language

The Health & Well-being Hub was founded in Logan Central by {{ site.founder_name }}, who saw first-hand how many Arabic-speaking, Somali, Afghan and Ethiopian families in Brisbane's diverse communities struggled to find an NDIS provider who understood their culture, faith and language. Culturally responsive support, not just translation

Speaking your language is only part of it. Our team also understands:

  • Gender-matched support worker preferences for cultural or religious reasons
  • Dietary requirements, including halal meal preparation
  • Prayer times and religious observances built into scheduling, not treated as a special request
  • Support to attend a mosque, church, temple or other place of worship as part of community participation
  • Cultural celebrations including Eid, Christmas and Diwali

A note on what this page is — and isn't

This page, and the rest of our website, is currently published in English only. We haven't published fully translated versions of our site in Arabic, Somali, Dari or Amharic, and we'd rather tell you that directly than imply otherwise. What we do have is team members who speak these languages and can talk you through your NDIS support directly, by phone, WhatsApp or in person at our Logan Central office.

If you'd prefer to start the conversation in your own language, call {{ site.phone_display }} or WhatsApp us and let us know your preferred language — we'll connect you with a team member who speaks it.
