{# Staff intake form. Same fields as the public referral form, because a referral that arrived by phone must produce the same record as one typed in by the referrer — same properties, same deal, same task, same acknowledgement email. Three things differ, and only three: 1. How it arrived, and who took it. A website referral needs neither. 2. The consent statement. Nobody can read a privacy policy on someone else's behalf, so the worker attests to what they told the referrer instead. See the endpoint's consent gate. 3. The wording throughout addresses the worker, not the referrer. data-form-name drives everything: main.js posts it as form_name, and the endpoint routes on 'staff_referral'. #}
