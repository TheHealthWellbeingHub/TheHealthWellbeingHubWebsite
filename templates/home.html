Our approach

Support that truly understands you

Every person is different. Every family is different. Every goal is different. That's why we never offer a one-size-fits-all approach — we take the time to understand who you are, what matters to you, and how we can best support you.

Our team comes from diverse backgrounds and speaks multiple languages including Arabic, Somali, Amharic and Dari. We understand cultural considerations, are sensitive to individual values and beliefs, and are committed to delivering support that feels right for you. Our story

Founded by community,
built for community

The Health & Well-being Hub was founded by {{ site.founder_name }} — a Brisbanite with more than 20 years of active community service and a lifelong passion for helping people in need.

{{ site.founder_name.split(' ')[0] }} saw how many families in Brisbane's Muslim community — Arabic-speaking, Somali, Afghan and Ethiopian families — were struggling to navigate NDIS without a provider who truly understood their culture, their faith, and their language. She built The Health & Well-being Hub to fill that gap. Read our full story.

"I started this because I believe everyone deserves support that truly sees them — their faith, their culture, their language, their goals."
