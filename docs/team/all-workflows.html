▸ 02 New enquiry acknowledgement Running
▸ Website enquiry form Nothing for you to do
1Automatic They fill in the enquiry form on the homepage or /contact/.Phone is required, email is optional.
2Automatic The system saves it. A contact record, a deal, and a job for the team due within 2 business hours.
3Automatic The moment the form registers — no one has to do anything.
4You A worker calls or replies.The job already has their number, or their email if that's all they gave.
▸ 03 New participant Ready to test
▸ Send the Consent email After the outcome call says yes
1You Tell Claude the participant wants to go ahead.Said after the outcome step already recorded a yes.
2Claude Both PDFs attach every time — the referral form and the NDIS consent form, never just one.
3You Confirm, and Claude sends it.From the H&W mailbox.
4Claude Claude leaves a note on the deal — a reminder to follow up if the forms don't come back.
▸ The forms are back Once both documents return
1You Tell Claude both forms are back.Only when both are in — a partial return isn't this trigger.
2Claude Claude reads the forms and writes a note. NDIS number, date of birth, plan details — onto the participant's own contact record.
3Claude Attaching the four easy-read guides.
4Automatic The deal moves to Participant Onboarded.
▸ 04 Maintaining participants Partly running
▸ Create, look up or update a record Look-up proven live this session
1You Tell Claude what you need.A new record, a lookup, or a change to an existing one.
2Claude Claude shows exactly what's about to change. Old value to new, before anything writes.
3You Confirm, and Claude writes it.
▸ Remove a record No real delete exists
1You Tell Claude to remove a participant or referrer.
2Claude Claude moves the deal to Lost / Not Suitable, or marks the referrer inactive.There's no delete tool — this is the safe equivalent.
▸ 05 Support worker introduction Ready to test
▸ Send the introduction email 5 steps · a couple of minutes
1You Tell Claude to send the introduction email, naming the participant.
2Claude Claude asks for what the template needs.Worker's name, role, experience, languages, first appointment details — nothing else.
3You Answer the questions.
4Claude
5Claude Claude leaves a note on the participant's contact — recording who was introduced and when.
▸ 06 Appointment confirmation Ready to test
▸ Set an appointment 6 steps · creates a calendar event too
1You Tell Claude to set an appointment, naming the participant.
2Claude Claude asks for the appointment details.Service, date, time, worker, location, prep notes.
3You Answer the questions.
4Claude Both shown before either is created.
5You Confirm, and both are created together.The participant is never added as a calendar guest — Google would email them separately, unbranded.
6Claude Claude leaves a note on the participant's contact.
▸ 07 Feedback and complaint acknowledgement Partly running
▸ Submit the form on Complaints & Feedback Live, except the acknowledgement email
1Automatic They fill in the form.Feedback or a complaint, what it's about, whether they want a reply. Phone and email are optional.
2Automatic The system saves it. A contact record and a note — no sales deal, this isn't that kind of step.
3Automatic A job is raised for the team.Complaints always get one. Feedback only if a reply was requested.
4Automatic HubSpot sends the acknowledgement — or , depending which they picked.Not live yet — until the HubSpot side is built, this raises a manual job instead.
▸ 08 Service cancellation and exit Ready to test
▸ Tell Claude a participant is exiting 5 steps · always a full exit
1You Tell Claude the participant is exiting, naming them.
2Claude Claude asks a short set of questions.Reason, final service date, final appointment if any, notice date, who's signing it. Not which service — every exit here is a full one.
3You Answer the questions.
4Claude
5Automatic The deal moves to Lost / Not Suitable.Always, for every exit.
