Why some of these are not automatic

Our email system can only start a workflow when someone fills in a form. Five of the eight below begin some other way — a plan being signed, an appointment booked, a service ending — so they need either a new form or Claude doing the step instead. That is the single reason most of this list is still open.

01 Partly running

Referral received

Starts whenSomeone refers a participant to us — three ways in.

The referrer is acknowledged, a job appears for the team on a two-hour clock, a worker calls the participant, and the referrer is told how it went.

02 Running

New enquiry acknowledgement

Starts whenSomeone fills in the enquiry form on the website.

They get an acknowledgement straight away and a job appears for the team. Running and confirmed working.

Two things to know: the wording has never been reviewed by Kholoud, and we have not confirmed it lands outside people's spam folders.

03 Not set up yet

New participant

Starts whenA participant agrees to come on board.

The Consent email is written and ready, and now has both documents a participant needs attached — a fillable referral form and the NDIA's own consent form, with real interactive fields rather than a flat PDF to print and post back.

Once the forms come back, the Welcome email hands over the welcome pack — four easy-read guides on privacy, feedback, rights and incident management. Built as template 12, ready the same way.

Neither is wired to a trigger, because signing an agreement is not something our email system can react to. Both can run the way workflow 01's outcome step does — tell Claude, and it sends from the H&W mailbox.

Walked through end to end as a dry run on 24 August 2026 — a fictional referral, through the staff intake link, to the outcome step, to both emails — and the mechanics hold: same confirm-before-send pattern as workflow 01, same mailbox send, the Consent email's two attachments fixed on every send. Nothing has gone to a real participant yet.

The dry run surfaced two real problems in workflow 01's outcome step, both fixed the same day: a referrer used to hear nothing when their referral said yes — now they get an email, the same as the other two outcomes — and the deal used to move to Lost / Not Suitable for a yes, which was a genuine bug. It now moves to Service Agreement Sent instead: active, not yet onboarded. See workflow 01 for both.

This is the one to settle first. Whatever we decide here decides workflows 04, 05 and 08 too.

04 Not set up yet

Maintaining participants

Starts whenNothing yet — this one runs on a rhythm, not an event.

The only one with nothing behind it at all, and the only one that repeats rather than firing once.

What it should actually do is genuinely undecided: plan review reminders, regular check-ins, agreement renewals, or getting back in touch with people who have gone quiet. They need different information and different timing.

Important Anything tied to plan dates or what a plan covers needs checking against our approved policies before it goes anywhere near a participant.
05 Not set up yet

Support worker introduction

Starts whenA support worker is matched to a participant.

The email is written and expects the worker's name, languages and a little about them. We do not keep support worker records, so there is nothing for it to fill in from.

Worth getting right rather than getting out — gender-matched workers and shared language are a large part of why families choose us.

06 Not set up yet

Appointment confirmation

Starts whenAn appointment is booked.

The email is written. The open question is where appointments actually get booked today, because that decides what the confirmation is built from.

It is also the only one with a natural second message — a reminder before the appointment, not just a confirmation after booking.

07 Not set up yet

Complaint acknowledgement

Starts whenSomeone makes a complaint.

Our complaints page explains how to complain but has no form on it. So nothing captures a complaint, nothing acknowledges it, and there is no record that one was made.

Of everything not yet set up, this is the one that is ready to build and the one with the most at stake.

Important NDIS complaint handling carries obligations about timeframes and what a person is told. The wording and the promised response time both need approval before this goes live.
08 Not set up yet

Service cancellation and exit

Starts whenA service ends.

The email is written. Same problem as workflow 03 — a service ending is not something our email system can react to.

The most sensitive email of the eight. It reaches someone as they leave, sometimes unhappily, and it is the last thing they will remember. Worth asking whether it should send automatically at all, or prompt a person to send it deliberately.

Important Exit wording touches notice periods and service agreement terms. Compliance review before it sends to anyone.

Two emails that sit outside the eight

Both are written and neither belongs to a workflow above.

Referrer introduction. Sent out to new referral partners rather than triggered by anything. Held up by email deliverability, not by anything unbuilt.
Feedback acknowledgement. The sibling of complaint acknowledgement, blocked by the same missing form. Build one form and both work.
