Our story

Founded by community,
built for community

The Health & Well-being Hub was founded by Kholoud Abdalla — a Brisbanite with more than 20 years of active community service and a lifelong passion for helping people in need.

Kholoud saw how many families in Brisbane's Muslim community — Arabic-speaking, Somali, Afghan and Ethiopian families — were struggling to navigate NDIS without a provider who truly understood their culture, their faith, and their language. She built The Health & Well-being Hub to fill that gap. Read our full story.

"I started this because I believe everyone deserves support that truly sees them — their faith, their culture, their language, their goals."
Kholoud Abdalla

Common questions

Everything you need to know

Can I switch from my current NDIS provider?
Yes — you have the right to change providers at any time. It's your plan and your choice. We can help make the transition seamless, often with no gap in support.
How quickly can I start?
In most cases we can begin support within the same week as your first enquiry. Call 0433 604 507 today.
Do you offer gender-matched support workers?
Yes. We understand the importance of this for many of our participants and families. We do our best to match participants with a support worker of their preferred gender.
Do you accommodate cultural and religious needs?
Yes. Our team is sensitive to all cultural and religious values. We offer gender-matched support workers, accommodate dietary requirements, respect religious observances and schedules, and work to ensure every participant feels seen and respected.
What languages do your team speak?
Our team speaks Arabic, Somali, Amharic, Dari and English. When you enquire, let us know your preferred language and we'll match you with a team member who speaks it.
What plan types do you work with?
We work with all NDIS plan types — agency managed, plan managed, and self managed.
Do you service my area?
We operate across QLD, NSW, VIC and WA. Call 0433 604 507 or submit an enquiry and we'll confirm coverage for your location.
Are you NDIS registered?
Yes — fully registered NDIS provider. Registration number: 4050045262. We meet all NDIS Practice Standards and are fully insured and compliant.
